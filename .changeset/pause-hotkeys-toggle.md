Add a global "Pause Hotkeys" shortcut that toggles the recording hotkeys on or off, so app shortcuts that share a chord (e.g. Option+Right in VLC) stop triggering recordings. Assign it in Settings → Hot Keys; the menu bar icon dims while paused and the menu gains a "Pause Recording Hotkeys" item. The paste-last-transcript shortcut and the toggle itself stay active, an in-flight recording can still be stopped normally, and the paused state resets on relaunch.
